The social platform for musical theatre creators.
StageLab is a mobile-first web app for performers, composers, lyricists, directors,  theatre makers, and Lovers of the Theatre  share work, 
find collaborators, track auditions, and connect with the local theatre community.
Features
🎬 Feed
	∙	TikTok-style swipeable performance feed
	∙	Like, comment, gift, and repost content
	∙	Subscribe to creators with Silver / Gold / Platinum tiers
	∙	View creator profiles and recent works
✂️ Create
	∙	Video — upload or record from camera, trim, filters, speed, custom thumbnail, publish to feed or save to profile
	∙	Audio — upload or record from mic with live waveform visualiser, playback preview
	∙	Poster Maker — live preview poster with 4 themes, custom background, cast & director fields, download or share
	∙	Lyrics — write with live styled card preview, accent colour picker, genre tags, co-writing toggle
🔍 Discover
	∙	People — search performers, composers, lyricists by skill and location. Follow and request collabs
	∙	Projects — browse open musical theatre projects seeking collaborators
	∙	Open Calls — auditions and collab calls with Paid / Remote badges
	∙	Local Shows — search by ZIP code for theatre happening near you
✦ Studio
	∙	AI Studio — Lyric Enhancer and Scene Builder powered by Claude AI with live streaming output
	∙	Casting — track auditions, self tapes, casting calls, and your résumé credits
	∙	Live — join live rehearsal rooms with real-time chat, emoji reactions, and mic/cam controls
👤 Me
	∙	Profile with cover photo and avatar upload
	∙	Photos & Media grid with lightbox viewer
	∙	Self Tapes tracker with status, preview, and share
	∙	Edit profile, Settings, Privacy controls
